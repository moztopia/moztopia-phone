# Community conduct

Be respectful, patient and constructive. Discuss technical evidence and tradeoffs without harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks. Respect privacy; do not publish personal information or real telephone-number datasets.

Maintainers may moderate or remove harmful contributions and restrict participation through the repository's normal human moderation process. Raise conduct concerns privately through an established maintainer contact; if no private channel is available, ask for one without posting sensitive details publicly.
